# Health Data Management & Deletion — Ferocity

**Updated:** October 17, 2026  
**Operator:** Foqsd LLC

Ferocity's optional health integration can connect to Android Health Connect or Apple Health. **Disconnecting, revoking permission and deleting saved information are different actions.**

## Stop future access

Open Ferocity's Health settings to disable connection or optional import/export features. You can also revoke Ferocity's permissions through Health Connect on Android or the Health app's access settings on iOS. Revoking permission prevents access requiring that permission, but does not automatically delete already-imported data or records stored by other providers.

## Remove an imported activity

For a standalone imported workout that offers **Remove from Ferocity**, open that activity and use the removal action. This removes its representation from Ferocity while leaving the original health-platform record intact. Ferocity may retain a local deletion marker so that the removed entry is not reimported.

## Delete source records

Use Apple Health, Health Connect or the originating health provider's tools to delete records held by that service. Removing a record within Ferocity does not delete it from Samsung Health, Apple Health or another source. Records exported by Ferocity may require separate management at the receiving health service.

## Remove local data

You can use available in-app controls for supported individual records. To erase Ferocity's local app storage, use your device's app-storage management controls where available. Removing the app also generally removes its app-private data from the device, but operating-system backups, exports and other stored copies can remain. Deleting local app storage may permanently remove your workout history, routines and preferences on that device.

## Backups and shared files

Ferocity's own portable backup excludes certain cached health measurements and provider identifiers; workout and profile information can remain in that backup. Device-level backups (including OS-managed backups) and any files you explicitly shared or exported are separate copies. Manage or delete those copies with the relevant device, backup provider or destination.

## Subscriptions

Removing app data or uninstalling Ferocity **does not cancel a subscription**. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play or your Apple account. Purchase records may be retained under store or service policies.

## Help and privacy requests

Contact **[ferocityapp@gmail.com](mailto:ferocityapp@gmail.com)** with your question or the action you want to take. Please avoid including sensitive health records unless necessary.
