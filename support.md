# Ferocity Support & Contact

**Ferocity** is operated by **Foqsd LLC**.

For app support, purchase questions, health integration help or privacy requests, contact **[ferocityapp@gmail.com](mailto:ferocityapp@gmail.com)**.

Please include your platform (Android or iOS), app version and a brief description of the problem where useful. Do not send passwords, full medical records or payment card numbers.

For subscription cancellation or refunds, start with your Google Play or Apple App Store purchase settings.
